Verdict

chunk200 is the clear winner for retrieval focus: Contextual Relevancy jumped from 0.24 → 0.46 (+0.22), the single largest improvement. Smaller chunks surface more targeted content and fewer off-topic sentences. Hallucination also improved (0.38 → 0.53), and Contextual Precision increased slightly (0.88 → 0.94).

chunk400 is better for faithfulness-sensitive answers: Faithfulness dropped from 0.79 → 0.67 (−0.12). Several simple factual questions (NSB-001, NSB-003, NSB-005, NSB-012, NSB-013, NSB-035) regressed from faith=1.00 to faith=0.00 in chunk200. These answers require synthesizing a figure plus its unit or context (e.g. "CAD 25,000 per business day") that spans slightly more than 200 characters — the model could not ground its response with truncated chunks.

Multi-hop category improved significantly with chunk200: Faithfulness 0.61 → 0.75, Correctness 0.81 → 1.00, Completeness 0.73 → 0.91. The critical NSB-022 failure (Dual Approval / same employee) was fully resolved — chunk200 retrieved the exact approval-policy chunk with high relevancy (0.83), enabling a faithful and correct answer.

Persistent failures unaffected by chunk size: NSB-023, NSB-024 (exception policy — "always Dual Approval", "cannot exceed CAD 10,000") and NSB-033 (Viewer vs User role distinction) fail under both configurations. These require either metadata-filtered retrieval targeting §1.1 and §3.2, or a prompt that explicitly instructs the model to apply negative constraints.

Analysis

chunk200 Wins

  • Ctx Relevancy +0.22 — biggest gain across all experiments. Smaller chunks reduce boilerplate noise around answers.
  • Hallucination +0.15 — focused chunks make it easier for the model to stay grounded in retrieved content.
  • Multi-hop correctness 0.81 → 1.00 — NSB-022 and NSB-015 both fixed; smaller chunks contain the specific rule without adjacent clutter.
  • Ctx Precision +0.06 — retrieving 3 small targeted chunks is more precise than 3 large mixed chunks.

chunk200 Regressions

  • Faithfulness −0.12 — answers requiring context that spans >200 chars lose grounding. The model generates correctly but the judge cannot verify claims against partial chunks.
  • 5 factual questions regressed (faith 1.00 → 0.00): limit values and procedural steps that need surrounding numeric context are split across chunk boundaries.
  • EFT cutoff questions (NSB-012/013) — settlement timing requires combining the cutoff time, the processing cycle, and the T+2 rule; chunk200 splits these into separate chunks that k=3 may not all retrieve.

Tradeoff Summary

  • chunk200: better for focused lookups and multi-hop reasoning where the answer fits within a single compact fact.
  • chunk400: better for answers that require surrounding context — unit + value + condition together in one chunk.
  • The faithfulness/relevancy tradeoff is fundamental to fixed-size chunking. A semantic chunker (split on sentence/section boundaries) would preserve both properties.
  • k=3 may be too low for chunk200 — increasing to k=5 could recover the missing context for factual regressions without losing the relevancy gains.

Persistent Failures (chunk-size independent)

  • NSB-023/024 (Exception) — implicit negative constraints ("always requires", "cannot exceed") not captured by embedding similarity. The model retrieves correct chunks but generates the wrong answer.
  • NSB-033 (Comparison) — Viewer vs User roles share similar embedding space; retrieval fails for both chunk sizes (prec=0.00, rec=0.00).
  • These require a non-retrieval fix: metadata-filtered retrieval (force §1.1 for role questions, §3.2 for approval questions) or a negative-constraint prompt.
  • Recommendation: Try k=5 with chunk200 next to recover factual recall, then address the 3 persistent failures via retrieval routing.
