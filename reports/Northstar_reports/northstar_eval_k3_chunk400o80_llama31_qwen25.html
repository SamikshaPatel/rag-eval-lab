Key Insights

Strengths

  • Perfect abstention (8/8): The model correctly refused all out-of-domain questions with no hallucinated responses.
  • Factual retrieval is reliable: Contextual Precision (0.88) and Recall (0.97) are consistently high across all categories — the chunking strategy retrieves the right content.
  • Factual & procedural categories excel: Faith=1.00, Corr=0.94, Comp=0.94. Simple lookups and step-by-step procedures are handled accurately.
  • Bias and Toxicity are perfect: All 36 answers scored 1.00 on both — the model produces safe, neutral responses throughout.

Failure Patterns

  • NSB-022 (Dual Approval, same employee): Faith=0.00, Corr=0.00 — the model failed to state the creator/approver must be different users.
  • NSB-023 (international wire Single Approval): Faith=0.00, Corr=0.00 — the model missed the "always Dual Approval regardless of amount" rule.
  • NSB-024 (threshold > CAD 10,000): Faith=0.00, Corr=0.00 — the model did not apply the maximum threshold constraint correctly.
  • NSB-033 (Viewer vs User): Prec=0.00, Rec=0.00, Faith=0.00, Corr=0.00 — total retrieval and generation failure for role-distinction question.

Exception & Multi-Hop Weakness

  • Exception category has the worst scores overall: Faith=0.33, Corr=0.33. Two of three exception questions (NSB-023, NSB-024) involve implicit negative constraints ("always requires", "cannot exceed") that the model conflates with positive retrieval.
  • Multi-hop faithfulness (0.61) drops significantly vs. factual (1.00). Reasoning across multiple retrieved chunks (e.g. EFT cutoff + settlement timing) introduces faithfulness degradation even when retrieval is perfect.
  • Comparison questions are vulnerable to role-disambiguation failures — NSB-033 drags the entire comparison category down to 0.65 correctness.

Contextual Relevancy Gap

  • Contextual Relevancy averages just 0.24 across all categories — this is the weakest metric overall and is consistent across factual (0.22), procedural (0.23), and multi-hop (0.27).
  • Even when the correct chunks are retrieved, the chunk-level relevancy score is low, suggesting the 400-char chunks contain surrounding boilerplate that dilutes relevancy.
  • Exception questions score the lowest relevancy (0.12), indicating retrieved context often includes adjacent policy text that is not directly applicable to the narrow exception case.
  • Next step: Experiment with smaller chunk sizes (200 chars) or metadata-filtered retrieval to improve chunk-level relevancy.
