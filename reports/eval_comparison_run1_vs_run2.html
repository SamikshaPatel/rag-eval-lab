Key Insights

The Chunk Size Trade-off

  • Smaller chunks (200) → higher contextual relevancy but lower faithfulness. The model sees more focused chunks but not enough context to answer completely.
  • Larger chunks (400) → lower relevancy but higher faithfulness. More context per chunk means the model can ground its answer, but chunks include off-topic sentences.
  • This is a fundamental RAG tension: chunk granularity vs. context sufficiency.
